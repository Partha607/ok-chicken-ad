The Northeast Market Opportunity

Northeast India is a rice-dominant market. Urban centers like Guwahati, Shillong, Imphal, Agartala, Kohima, Aizawl, Itanagar, and Gangtok have a massive base of students and office workers seeking value-for-money daily meals.

Strategic Goal

Position OK Chicken Rice Bowl as the "Everyday Affordable Rice Meal of Northeast India." Low risk. High volume. Fast ROI.
